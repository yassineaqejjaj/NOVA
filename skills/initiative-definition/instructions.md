# Initiative Definition — method

- Ground the problem, objectives and constraints in the user's input and the provided context; cite
  labels (`S1`…) in `citations`. Proposals carry a one-sentence `rationale`.
- The initiative is a bet: state the expected impact and the confidence behind it honestly.
- Scope items are capabilities users or the business get, not engineering tasks.
- Milestones should each produce something demonstrable or a learning; avoid "development done".
- Do not invent dates, budgets, owners, baselines or decisions. Leave unknown fields empty.
- If the initiative conflicts with a decision or objective in context, say so in the problem section.
- Keep ids short and stable (`obj-1`, `metric-activation`, `ms-beta`, `dep-billing`).
