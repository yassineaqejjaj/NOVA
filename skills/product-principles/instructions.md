# Product Principles — method

- Derive principles from the vision, strategy, research and past decisions in the provided context; cite
  labels (`S1`…) in `citations`. A principle you propose carries a one-sentence `rationale`.
- Reject platitudes ("be user-centric", "quality matters"): if nobody would argue for the opposite, it is
  not a principle.
- Phrase each principle as a trade-off ("X over Y") or an equally explicit preference.
- Each principle should be traceable to at least one example decision it would settle.
- Example decisions taken from context keep their real outcome; never invent decisions as if they were
  taken, nor names or dates.
- Keep ids short and stable (`p-clarity`, `p-2`, `ex-1`).
