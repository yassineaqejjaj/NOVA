# Risk Analysis — method

- Ground risks in the provided context (plans, incidents, decisions, constraints) and cite labels (`S1`…)
  in `citations`. Risks from the pre-mortem without evidence carry a one-sentence `rationale`.
- A risk is an uncertain event with a consequence ("If the vendor API changes its limits, imports will
  fail for large accounts"), not a vague worry ("technical risk").
- Rate honestly: avoid marking everything high; justify high ratings.
- Mitigations are concrete actions that reduce likelihood or impact, not "monitor closely".
- Owners are roles unless a person is named in context. Never invent incidents, dates or decisions.
- Keep ids short and stable (`risk-1`, `asm-2`, `act-3`).
