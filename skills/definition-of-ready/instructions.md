# Definition of Ready — method

- Start from the team's existing agreements, retrospective notes and recurring blockers in the provided
  context; cite labels (`S1`…) in `citations`. Generic criteria carry a one-sentence `rationale`.
- Each criterion is binary: someone can answer yes or no in refinement.
- Keep it short; more than 10 criteria turns readiness into bureaucracy.
- Avoid criteria that require all design or all answers upfront; aim for "enough to start".
- If an existing Definition of Ready is in context, improve it and keep the ids of retained items.
- Do not invent team names, people or past incidents.
