# User Story Generation — method

- Derive stories from the PRD, requirements, decisions and personas in the provided context; cite their
  labels (`S1`…) in `citations`. Stories you propose beyond the source carry a one-sentence `rationale`.
- Write stories for real roles ("as a workspace admin"), never "as a user" when a better role exists,
  and never "as a developer" for technical tasks.
- Each story must be independently valuable and small enough for a few days of work.
- Acceptance criteria are observable behaviour with concrete values; no "should work well".
- When a business rule is unknown, add an open question rather than inventing the rule.
- Do not invent estimates, names or decisions. Prefer 8–15 sharp stories over 40 vague ones.
- Keep ids short and stable (`epic-export`, `story-3`, `q-1`).
