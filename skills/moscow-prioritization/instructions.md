# MoSCoW Prioritization — method

- Take items, the release goal and constraints from the user's input or the provided context; cite labels
  (`S1`…) in `citations` on the items they inform.
- Be honest with Must: if a workaround exists, it is a Should. A list where most items are Must is wrong.
- Won't have is valuable: it records what stakeholders should not expect in this timebox.
- Regulatory or contractual obligations found in context are Must — cite them.
- When estimates exist, compute the Must share; otherwise say it cannot be checked. Never invent estimates.
- Each item has a one-sentence `rationale` for its bucket.
- Keep item ids stable and reuse the ids or keys of items found in context.
