# Stakeholder Brief — method

- Ground the topic, timeline, impacted teams and decisions in the provided context; cite labels (`S1`…) in
  `citations`. Stakeholder assessments you infer carry a one-sentence `rationale`.
- Describe stakeholders by role or group; use personal names only when they appear in context, and never
  add contact details.
- Messages answer, for each audience: what changes for me, when, and what do you need from me?
- Engagement follows the grid: high influence and high interest → manage closely.
- Never invent decisions, dates or organizational facts.
- Keep ids short and stable (`sh-sales`, `msg-1`, `ask-1`).
