# Product Update — method

- Report only progress, metrics, risks and decisions found in the provided context; cite labels (`S1`…)
  in `citations`. If information is missing for the period, say so rather than filling the gap.
- The headline states the status honestly; "at risk" early is better than a surprise later.
- Outcomes before outputs: "activation +4 pts after the new onboarding" beats "shipped onboarding v2".
- Asks are specific and actionable; no ask is better than a vague one.
- Write for the stated audience: less jargon and fewer ticket numbers for leadership.
- Never invent figures, dates, names or decisions. Keep it under one page.
