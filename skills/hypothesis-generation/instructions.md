# Hypothesis Generation — method

- Ground the idea, outcome and existing evidence in the user's input or the provided context; cite
  labels (`S1`…) in `citations`. Each proposed assumption or hypothesis carries a one-sentence `rationale`.
- A hypothesis must be able to fail: "users will like it" is not testable; "at least 30% of invited
  admins enable the feature within 14 days" is.
- Thresholds are proposals, not facts — base them on baselines in context when they exist and say so.
- Riskiest = most important to the idea's success and least supported by evidence.
- Prior experiment results in context lower uncertainty: reflect them in confidence and cite them.
- Do not invent baselines, results, names or decisions.
- Keep ids short and stable (`asm-1`, `hyp-1`).
