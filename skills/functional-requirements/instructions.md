# Functional Requirements — method

- Derive requirements from the PRD, stories, business rules and decisions in the provided context; cite
  labels (`S1`…) in `citations`. Requirements you add carry a one-sentence `rationale`.
- One requirement = one behaviour. Split "and" requirements.
- State what the system does, not how (no tables, frameworks or endpoints unless a decision imposes them).
- Avoid ambiguous words ("fast", "user-friendly", "etc."); quality attributes belong to non-functional
  requirements, not here.
- Use MoSCoW honestly; not everything is a must.
- When a rule is unknown, add an open question rather than inventing it. Never invent decisions or names.
- Keep ids short and stable (`req-export-1`, `con-1`, `q-2`).
