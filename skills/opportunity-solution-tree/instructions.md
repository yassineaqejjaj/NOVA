# Opportunity Solution Tree — method

- Take the outcome from objectives in the provided context when available and cite it (`S1`…).
  Opportunities must cite the research they come from; proposed solutions carry a `rationale`.
- An opportunity phrased as a feature ("add a dashboard") is a solution — reframe the underlying need.
- Keep the tree strict with `parent_id`: opportunity → solution → experiment.
- Generate genuinely different solutions for a target opportunity, not variations of one idea.
- Experiments are small and fast (prototype, fake door, one-question survey), not full builds.
- Do not invent research findings, metrics or results.
- Keep ids short and stable (`opp-1`, `opp-1-sol-2`, `exp-3`).
