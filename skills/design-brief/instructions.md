# Design Brief — method

- Ground the content in the provided context and cite labels (`S1`…) in `citations`; items without evidence carry a one-sentence `rationale`.
- Do not invent research findings; say when evidence is missing.
- Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) is always a constraint.
- Owners are roles unless a person is named in context. Never invent facts, figures, dates or decisions.
