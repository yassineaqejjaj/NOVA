# User Research Plan — method

- Ground the decision, prior knowledge and target segments in the user's input or the provided context;
  cite labels (`S1`…) in `citations`. Proposed choices carry a one-sentence `rationale`.
- Do not plan research to confirm what is already known: reuse existing insights and cite them.
- Research questions are learning goals; they must be answerable by the chosen method.
- Prefer behaviour (what people did) over opinion (what they say they would do) when choosing methods.
- Recruiting criteria describe behaviours and context; never include personal data or real names.
- Do not invent dates, budgets, participant counts already agreed, or decisions.
- Keep ids short and stable (`rq-1`, `ms-recruit`, `risk-bias-1`).
