# WSJF Prioritization — method

- Take candidates, deadlines, risks and estimates from the user's input or the provided context; cite
  labels (`S1`…) in `citations` on the items they inform.
- Score each Cost of Delay component column by column across all items (relative), not item by item.
- Time criticality needs a reason: a fixed date, a market window, or value that decays — cite it.
- Job size is relative effort; reuse estimates from context when they exist and say when you guessed.
- Check the arithmetic: (8 + 5 + 3) ÷ 8 = 2.0.
- Never invent deadlines, estimates or commitments; each item has a one-sentence `rationale`.
- Keep item ids stable and reuse the ids or keys of items found in context.
