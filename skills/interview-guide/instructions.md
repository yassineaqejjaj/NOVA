# Interview Guide — method

- Derive questions from the research goal and the research questions or hypotheses in the provided
  context; cite their labels (`S1`…) in `citations`. Give a one-sentence `rationale` for each core
  question that is your own proposal.
- Every core question must be open and about the past: no "would you…", no "do you like…", no yes/no.
- Never describe or pitch the product idea in a question; it biases the answers.
- Keep the guide to what fits the session length (about 5 minutes per core question with probes).
- Do not ask for personal data that the research does not need; the consent text must mention recording.
- Do not invent prior findings, participant names or decisions.
- Keep ids short and stable (`warm-1`, `core-3`, `probe-3a`).
