# Executive Summary — method

- Summarize only what the provided context supports; cite labels (`S1`…) in `citations` on key points,
  decisions and risks. Your recommendation carries a one-sentence `rationale`.
- Answer first: the first sentence a busy executive reads must contain the conclusion.
- Choose the few points that matter for the decision; drop interesting but non-essential details.
- Plain language, no internal acronyms, numbers with units and timeframes.
- Present real alternatives in decisions needed, including "do nothing" when relevant.
- Never invent figures, deadlines, names or decisions. Keep it to one page.
