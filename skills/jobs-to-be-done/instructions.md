# Jobs to Be Done — method

- Derive jobs from research in the provided context (interviews, switching stories, feedback) and cite
  labels (`S1`…) in `citations`. Jobs you infer carry a one-sentence `rationale`.
- Jobs are solution-agnostic: "When I prepare the monthly report, I want to trust the numbers…" — not
  "I want to use the export button".
- The situation is a concrete circumstance (trigger, context), not a persona description.
- Desired outcomes are measurable from the customer's view (time, effort, errors, uncertainty), without
  naming a solution.
- Alternatives include non-consumption and manual workarounds, not only competitors.
- Do not invent quotes, frequencies, metrics or competitor facts.
- Keep ids short and stable (`job-main`, `out-1`, `strug-2`, `alt-spreadsheet`).
