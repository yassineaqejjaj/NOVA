# Vision to Objectives — method

- Read the vision first from the provided context; cite its label (`S1`…) on the summary and on every
  objective it supports. If no vision is available, say so in the summary.
- Keep the chain explicit: vision → objective → key result / initiative, linked with `parent_id`.
- Objectives are qualitative and motivating; key results are quantitative and verifiable.
- A key result phrased as a deliverable ("launch the new onboarding") is an initiative — move it.
- Do not invent baselines, targets presented as facts, dates, owners or decisions. When a baseline is
  unknown, leave it empty and add an assumption to measure it early.
- Proposed items carry a one-sentence `rationale`; items taken from context carry `citations`.
- Prefer 2–3 sharp objectives over a long list.
- Keep ids short and stable (`obj-1`, `kr-1-2`, `init-onboarding`).
