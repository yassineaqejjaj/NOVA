# PR/FAQ — method

- Ground the customer problem, the market and the constraints in the user's input or the provided
  context; cite labels (`S1`…) in `citations` on FAQ answers. Proposals carry a one-sentence `rationale`.
- Write for the customer: no internal jargon, no technology names unless customers care about them.
- The press release is short (one page); the benefit must be specific and believable.
- The customer quote is explicitly hypothetical and attributed to a role, never to a real or realistic
  named person.
- Internal FAQs must include the hardest questions (why might this fail? what does it cost?), answered
  honestly or marked as open.
- Do not invent prices, dates, market sizes, metrics or partner names.
- Keep ids short and stable (`efaq-1`, `ifaq-why-now`).
