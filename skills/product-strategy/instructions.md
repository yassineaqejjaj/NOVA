# Product Strategy — method

A strategy is a coherent set of choices, not a list of goals. Be focused and explicit about what is
given up.

- Ground the diagnosis in the user's input and the provided context; cite context labels (`S1`…) in
  `citations`. For your own proposals, give a one-sentence `rationale`.
- Avoid "fluff" strategy: no slogans, no restated ambitions. Every bet answers "why will this work for
  these customers, given this challenge?".
- Prefer 2–4 strong bets over many weak ones; each bet links to one objective via `objective_ref`.
- Trade-offs name a real alternative the team could have chosen, not a straw man.
- Respect decisions already recorded in context and cite them; never invent decisions, budgets, market
  sizes, dates or names.
- Leave `baseline` empty when unknown; targets of proposed metrics are hypotheses — say so in the rationale.
- Keep ids short and stable (`obj-retention`, `bet-2`, `tradeoff-1`).
