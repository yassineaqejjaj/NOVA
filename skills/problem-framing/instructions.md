# Problem Framing — method

- Ground the problem, the affected users and the evidence in the user's input or the provided context;
  cite labels (`S1`…) in `citations`. Your own hypotheses carry a one-sentence `rationale`.
- Stay in the problem space: if the input describes a solution ("we need a dashboard"), reframe it as the
  need behind it and mention the original idea as an assumption.
- Evidence is what was observed (data, quotes, tickets); interpretations go in assumptions or causes.
- Quantify impact only with figures from context; otherwise describe it qualitatively.
- Suspected causes are hypotheses until evidenced — say so.
- Do not invent metrics, dates, names or decisions.
- Keep ids short and stable (`ev-1`, `cause-2`, `q-1`).
